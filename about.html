About Our Practice

At Scarlett May, fashion is treated as an artistic practice rather than simply a product. Founder, Scarlett Mandić is driven by emotion, instinct and most importantly her strong intuition.

The practice of “Scarlett May” will never be about just “making more clothes”. Pieces from the brand become a way of making internal worlds physical, through an obsession to find unusual materials, using only dead stock & reclaimed materials, from old furnishings, and fabrications that have a history, to create a narrative through each of the unique pieces, hoping to make an experience for each viewer rather, that really tell a story.

“I am here to create an experience, a performance, a new world.” - Scarlett Mandić

Our Story

“Scarlett May” founded by Scarlett Mandić, is a forever evolving artistic lead practice born from a deeply emotional and and forever restless mind.

Behind my first collection, comes from an obsession to understand the psychological mind and understanding how each individual flows through this world. Being highly inspired by overlooked characters throughout history, I have taken them as reference throughout my debut collection. “Scarlett May” strives to create a world that is slightly strange, bringing light to the overlooked and underestimated, to make my viewers feel and push beyond the perceptions of others.
